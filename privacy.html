Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

DocuDiscard is committed to protecting the privacy of restoration professionals and their clients. This policy outlines how we handle data within the app.

1. Data Collection and Storage

We believe in data ownership. All job details, client information, and item photos are stored locally on your device or within your private iCloud storage. We do not host your claim data on our own servers. This app allows users to provide their own Gemini API keys. These keys are stored locally on your device and are used only to communicate directly with Google's servers. We do not store, view, or collect your API keys or the content of your AI prompts on our own servers.

2. AI Analysis & Third-Party Data Sharing

The "Analyze with AI" feature sends data to Google’s Gemini AI service (operated by Google LLC). Before any data is transmitted, the app displays a consent notice and requires your explicit permission.

When you grant permission and initiate an analysis, the following data is sent directly from your device to Google’s Gemini API:

  • Photos of your items (when using photo analysis)
  • Text descriptions of your items (when using text-based analysis)
  • Your personal Gemini API key (transmitted securely via HTTPS directly to Google — never to our servers)

This data is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms of Service. Google provides equivalent data protection standards compliant with applicable privacy laws.

We do not store, view, or collect your photos, descriptions, API keys, or AI analysis results on our own servers. You may revoke your consent at any time by not using the AI analysis feature.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

All premium subscriptions are processed and managed through RevenueCat In-App Purchases.This handles your purchase history anonymously to unlock features. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or billing addresses. Your transaction data is managed entirely by RevenueCat.

4. Data Security

Since data is stored locally, the security of your documentation depends on your device's security settings (Passcode, FaceID, and iCloud encryption). We recommend using device encryption to protect sensitive claim information.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: docudiscard@gmail.com
